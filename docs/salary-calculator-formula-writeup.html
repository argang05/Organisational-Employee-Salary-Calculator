Salary Calculator Formula Writeup

This document describes the formulas, caps, tax logic, validations, and assumptions currently used in the salary calculator and the appraisal comparison calculator.

Important: This writeup reflects the implemented app logic in code, including later revisions such as dynamic car perks, retirement-contribution tax treatment, surcharge handling, and separate comparison-year rules.

1. Global Constants

Item Value / Rule
Standard deduction Rs. 75,000 annually
BYOD benefit Rs. 1,500 monthly when opted
Professional tax (main calculator) Rs. 2,500 / 12 monthly when opted
Professional tax (comparison 2025-26) Rs. 200 monthly, always applied
Professional tax (comparison 2026-27) round(Rs. 2,500 / 12) monthly, always applied

2. Rounding Rules

  • round(x) means normal rounding to the nearest whole number.
  • round2(x) means rounding to two decimal places.
  • Net annual tax is rounded upward using ceiling logic after adding cess and surcharge.
  • Invalid or negative money inputs are sanitized to 0.

3. Main Salary Calculator

3.1 Input Sanitization

  • If fixed annual CTC is blank, zero, invalid, or negative, the calculator returns a full zeroed output.
  • Car rental, car perks, VPF, and loans/advances are clamped so they never start below 0.

3.2 Salary Structure

Component Formula
Monthly CTC ceil(Annual CTC / 12)
Basic round(max(Monthly CTC x 50%, Rs. 17,000))
HRA round(Basic x 40%)
LTA round(Basic x 10%)
Bonus Allowance round(Basic x 8.33%) if annual CTC is below Rs. 5,04,000 or monthly basic is below Rs. 21,000; otherwise 0
BYOD Rs. 1,500 monthly when selected; otherwise 0
Car Perks User-entered monthly amount when car rental is selected; otherwise 0

3.3 Employer Contributions

Component Formula
NPS round2(Basic x NPS%) where NPS option can be 0%, 5%, 10%, 12.5%, or 14%
PF 0, Rs. 3,000, or round2(Basic x 12%) depending on user selection
Gratuity round(Basic x 4.81%)
Total Employer Contribution round2(NPS + PF + Gratuity)

3.4 Special Allowance and Car Rental

Base special allowance is calculated as:

Monthly CTC - Basic - HRA - LTA - Bonus - Car Perks - Total Employer Contribution

Car rental handling:

  • Max allowed car rental = 95% of base special allowance.
  • If user-entered car rental exceeds that cap, it is reduced to the cap and a warning is shown.
  • Final special allowance before optional deductions: Base Special Allowance - Car Rental Amount
  • Negative special allowance is allowed and remains negative. It also reduces gross salary accordingly and is shown as a warning state in the UI.
  • Remaining car rental shown in the UI = max(0, Car Rental Amount - Car Perks)

3.5 Gross Salary and CTC Checks

Component Formula
Gross Salary Basic + HRA + LTA + Bonus Allowance + Special Allowance
Calculated Monthly CTC Gross Salary + Total Employer Contribution
Net Diff Monthly CTC - Calculated Monthly CTC

3.6 Total Retiral Contribution and Additional Taxable Income

  • Total retiral contribution = round((NPS + PF) x 12)
  • Additional taxable income = max(0, Total Retiral Contribution - Rs. 7,50,000)
  • This additional taxable income is used internally in tax calculation but is not separately displayed to the user.

3.7 Net Taxable Income

The implemented main-calculator tax base is:

max(0, ((Gross Salary + BYOD + Car Perks) x 12) + Additional Taxable Income - Rs. 75,000)

3.8 Income Tax, Surcharge, and Cess

If net taxable income is at or below Rs. 12,00,000, slab tax is 0.

Otherwise slab tax is computed as follows:

Income Slice Rate
0 - 4,00,0000%
4,00,001 - 8,00,0005%
8,00,001 - 12,00,00010%
12,00,001 - 16,00,00015%
16,00,001 - 20,00,00020%
20,00,001 - 24,00,00025%
Above 24,00,00030%

Surcharge is applied on slab tax using these thresholds:

Net Taxable Income Surcharge Rate
Up to 50,00,0000%
50,00,001 - 1,00,00,00010%
1,00,00,001 - 2,00,00,00015%
Above 2,00,00,00025%

Final tax formulas:

  • Tax With Surcharge = Base Tax x (1 + Surcharge Rate)
  • Education Cess = Tax With Surcharge x 4%
  • Net Annual Tax = ceil(Tax With Surcharge + Education Cess)
  • Net Monthly Tax = Net Annual Tax / 12

3.9 Net In Hand and Optional Deductions

Base deductions:

PF + Net Monthly Tax + Professional Tax

Important: car rental is not included in total deductions.

Net in hand before optional deductions:

Gross Salary + BYOD - Base Deductions

Optional Item Formula / Rule
VPF Deducted only from net in hand. Max allowed is min(Basic, Net In Hand Before Optional Deductions).
Medical Insurance 0, Rs. 833, or Rs. 1,667 monthly depending on chosen tier; deducted only from net in hand.
Loans and Advances Deducted only from net in hand. Max allowed is the smaller of: 70% of net in hand before optional deductions, or the remaining amount after VPF and medical insurance.

Total deductions:

PF + Net Monthly Tax + Professional Tax + VPF + Medical Insurance + Loans and Advances

Final net in hand:

max(0, Gross Salary + BYOD - Total Deductions)

4. Comparison Calculator

The comparison calculator has two separately configurable columns: 2025-26 and 2026-27.

4.1 Rules Common to Both Comparison Columns

  • Monthly CTC = ceil(Annual CTC / 12)
  • HRA = round(Basic x 40%)
  • LTA = round(Basic x 10%)
  • Bonus = round(Basic x 8.33%) if annual CTC is below Rs. 5,04,000 or basic is below Rs. 21,000
  • Gratuity = round(Basic x 4.81%)
  • BYOD = Rs. 1,500 monthly when selected
  • Car perks are user-entered when car rental is selected; otherwise 0
  • Car rental max cap = 95% of pre-rental special allowance
  • Remaining car rental shown in UI = max(0, Car Rental Amount - Car Perks)
  • Total retiral contribution = round((NPS + PF) x 12)
  • Additional taxable income = max(0, Total Retiral Contribution - Rs. 7,50,000)
  • Net taxable income = max(0, ((Gross Salary + BYOD + Car Perks) x 12) + Additional Taxable Income - Rs. 75,000)
  • Surcharge and cess logic follow the same rules as the main calculator
  • VPF, medical insurance, and loans/advances are deducted from net salary, not from special allowance
  • Net salary is never allowed to become negative
  • Car rental is not included in employee deduction totals

4.2 Comparison 2026-27 Rules

Item Formula / Rule
Basic round(max(Monthly CTC x 50%, Rs. 17,000))
PF 0, Rs. 3,000, or 12% of Basic
NPS options 0%, 5%, 10%, 12.5%, 14%
Professional tax round(Rs. 2,500 / 12), mandatory

4.3 Comparison 2025-26 Rules

Item Formula / Rule
Eligibility If annual CTC is below Rs. 3,50,000, this side is not calculated and a warning is shown.
Basic round(max(Monthly CTC x 30%, Rs. 16,000))
PF Always round(Basic x 12%)
NPS options 0%, 10%, 14%
Professional tax Rs. 200, mandatory

4.4 Comparison Employee Deduction and Net Salary

Employee deduction in comparison is:

PF + Professional Tax + Monthly Income Tax + VPF + Medical Insurance + Loans and Advances

Final comparison net salary is:

max(0, Gross Salary + BYOD - Employee Deduction)

5. Validation and Warning Logic

  • Negative or invalid money inputs are reset to 0.
  • Car rental is capped at 95% of special allowance base.
  • VPF is capped at the allowed in-hand / basic-based maximum.
  • Loans and advances are capped at the lesser of available in-hand amount and 70% of in-hand amount.
  • Special allowance may go negative in the main calculator and that is intentional.
  • Net in hand / net salary is always prevented from becoming negative.
  • If caps are applied, the UI shows warning messages and uses the capped values in the final calculations.

6. Key Implementation Assumptions

  • The calculator follows the latest implemented logic, even where earlier business notes changed over time.
  • Car perks are dynamic and are treated as taxable only when car rental is enabled.
  • Car rental is displayed separately for clarity and is not counted inside total deductions / employee deductions.
  • Additional taxable income from retiral contribution is applied internally to tax calculations and not shown as a separate visible figure.
  • The comparison calculator is a side-by-side monthly comparison view and uses year-specific rules where instructed.
